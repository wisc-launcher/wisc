Fonts

Large headings use Minecraftia by Andrew Tyler, served from the website’s own assets. Read the font attribution and embedded license notice and its original readme.

Body text and controls use Inter by Rasmus Andersson, bundled locally under the SIL Open Font License. Large headings use Minecraftia.

The desktop launcher uses Pixelify Sans under the SIL Open Font License.

The desktop launcher’s original Mojangles notices are also retained in the font license and font readme.
