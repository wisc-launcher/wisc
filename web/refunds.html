WISC / REFUNDS

Free to download.

Wisc Launcher has no paid downloads, subscriptions, or purchases on this website.

No project checkout

There is no checkout or billing system here, so the website has no Wisc purchase to refund. Official project downloads are linked from the download page and hosted on GitHub.

Purchases elsewhere

Minecraft purchases, third-party services, and purchases from other sellers are governed by those sellers’ refund policies. Contact the seller where the payment occurred. If a third party charges for a package, its transaction is separate from this website.
