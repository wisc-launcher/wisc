### Added

- Open Skins in the Qt launcher with an interactive character preview, drag and keyboard rotation, and 3D, Front, and Back views.
- Manage your skin on Ely.by and refresh it in the launcher, with the character model supplied automatically by Ely.by.

### Changed

- Simplified Skins with rounded frosted glass panels, a player avatar, theme-colored actions, and concise loading and error feedback.
- Offline accounts show their saved appearance and a prompt to sign in with Ely.by to change their skin.

### Removed

- Local skin and cape PNG choosers and manual Classic/Slim model controls from the Qt Skins tab.
